## Intent

A client-facing showcase of mazad.alfrusiyaar.com told entirely through the
CLIENT's eyes — no admin frames. It follows one signed-in member (سلطان
القحطاني, a demo account created for this video) as he browses curated horses,
leads a live auction with a real bid, offers his own horse for sale, and
manages his requests, bids, and purchases from his account page. Tone:
premium, trustworthy, equestrian — af brand (cream + teal, Alexandria type).

## Assets

- Local site http://localhost:3100 — captures taken signed-in AS the showcase client (header shows his name).
- /logo.svg, /pattern.svg — brand marks.
- Brand tokens: teal #0E5F82, navy #2f3382, orange #f5730f, crimson #7d1300, cream #f6f3ec, ink #191510; Alexandria font.

## Customizations

- Show-it-as-is: captured screens as the video's assets — home, marketplace, live auction room (client leading at 170,000), horse card with a real photo, sell form, and the account dashboard (طلبات البيع / مزايداتي / صفقاتي).
- NO admin screens anywhere.
- Silent film: no narration, no BGM — Arabic on-screen typography carries it.
- Real horse photographs appear inside the captured screens (cards + auction panel).
